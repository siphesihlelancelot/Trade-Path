LOCAL CONNECTION

Connect in a few steps.

Choose the read-only Expert Advisor for your terminal. It sends recent chart candles to a bridge on this computer. No broker login or account data is collected.

  1. 01Run node bridge.mjs from the TradePath folder.
  2. 02Open the local app link printed in the terminal.
  3. 03In your terminal, allow http://127.0.0.1:8765 in Tools → Options → Expert Advisors.
  4. 04Compile and attach TradePathMonitor.mq5 (MT5) or TradePathMonitor.mq4 (MT4) to a chart. Enter the terminal token in the EA's InpBridgeToken input.
What is shared and what is not?

Each EA sends the platform, chart symbol, timeframe, and up to 120 recent closed OHLC candles to a server bound to this computer only. The bridge keeps up to 64 chart feeds. It never reads account balances, positions, passwords, or places/modifies trades. Keep the token private and remove the EA or stop the bridge to disconnect.

Chart analysis is educational and may be delayed or incomplete. It is not a trade signal, financial advice, or a recommendation to buy or sell. The app cannot choose a "best" strategy or guarantee results.
